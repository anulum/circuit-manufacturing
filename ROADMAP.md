# Roadmap

## Current foundation

**Being qualified:**
- repository identity and legal texts;
- architecture and governance;
- native documentation;
- dedicated validation tools.

**Domain capabilities and claims:** empty.

## Capability milestones

1. **The thirteen planned v1 record schemas with golden fixtures** (`manufacturing_package` through `corrective_action_and_rma_record`).
2. **In-process `STDF v4` (`Semi-ATE/STDF`) and chunk-sealed `SEMI E183 RITdb` parsers** with one-sided `Cpk` lower-confidence-bound and `WAT`/`PCM` extractors.
3. **Moisture floor-life, `GHS` waste-compatibility, anti-counterfeit BOM and sibling-lot `RMA` hold validators.**

**Enrolled with every implementation change:**
- strict native typing and linting;
- native documentation;
- meaningful statement and branch coverage;
- affected boundary tests and benchmarks.

**Requires its own evidence and explicit approval:**
- every supplier handoff, recipe release, work order and material disposition;
- adaptive test bin changes;
- public activation and release.

No availability date is promised. No performance, yield or productivity gain is promised either.
